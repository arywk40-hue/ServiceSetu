# ServiceSetu concierge

![tag:innovationlab](https://img.shields.io/badge/innovationlab-3D8BD3)
![tag:hackathon](https://img.shields.io/badge/hackathon-5F43F1)

Book a fictional AC servicing or plumbing visit in Indiranagar, Bengaluru. Compare provider agents, see honest trust labels, approve one quote, receive a test booking, and recover from provider cancellation. Try: AC service in Indiranagar Bengaluru tomorrow 10am under INR 1000. Ask show what happened for an audit timeline. Providers, credentials and registry are seeded test data. Payments are simulated or test-only; native FET is restricted to Dorado testnet and mainnet payments are refused. Discretionary refunds, payouts and verification overrides require human review.
