# ServiceSetu concierge

![tag:innovationlab](https://img.shields.io/badge/innovationlab-3D8BD3)
![tag:hackathon](https://img.shields.io/badge/hackathon-5F43F1)

Book a fictional AC servicing or plumbing visit in Indiranagar, Bengaluru. Compare provider agents, see honest trust labels, approve one quote, receive a test booking, and recover from provider cancellation. Try: AC service in Indiranagar Bengaluru tomorrow 10am under INR 1000. Ask show what happened for an audit timeline. Providers, credentials and registry are seeded test data. Payment is simulated or Stripe test mode; no real service or money. Discretionary refunds, payouts and verification overrides require human review.
